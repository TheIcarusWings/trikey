## Nostr note

```
Building Trikey: a 2-of-3 bitcoin wallet that's as easy to use as any app, with no company holding a key.

🔑 Key 1: any hardware wallet (Ledger, Trezor, Coldcard, BitBox, Jade…). Big payments, rule changes, recovery.
📱 Key 2: your phone, backed up to your own cloud. Everyday payments with Face ID.
🏠 Key 3: Trikey Guardian, an app on your own Umbrel or Start9. Co-signs phone payments under a daily limit.

Any two keys move money. The wallet is a plain wsh(sortedmulti(2,…)) descriptor, so any multisig wallet can recover it without Trikey.

Phone and guardian talk over end-to-end encrypted nostr relays. Outbound only, no ports opened at home, no company servers.

Pre-alpha and not audited. Testnet only. Code, design doc and threat model, MIT licensed:
https://trikey.app
Code: https://github.com/TheIcarusWings/trikey

#bitcoin #nostr #umbrel #startos #plebchain
```
