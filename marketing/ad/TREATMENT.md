# Trikey brand ads: treatment

Status: pitch. Nothing generated yet.

The explainers (V1–V3) teach the mechanics. These ads sell the feeling: your money, kept in your own
life, with nobody in the middle. Cinematic footage from Higgsfield carries the story; supers carry the
words; the logo animation from the brand cut (`v1b-brand`) closes every ad.

## Shared rules

- **People:** fictional and mostly faceless (hands, backs, silhouettes, out-of-focus). One protagonist
  wardrobe across shots for continuity: dark green wool coat, grey knit sweater. No real people, no
  celebrity look-alikes.
- **Devices:** generic and unbranded. Hardware wallet = "small matte black device with a tiny screen and
  two buttons". Home server = "small grey box with one soft white LED". Phones are plain black slabs,
  screens dark or out of focus. Real Trikey UI only appears as inserts rendered from `design/screens`.
- **Look:** natural light, muted palette, shallow depth of field, slow camera. Warm orange appears
  once, in the logo's top dot at the end.
- **Words:** supers in Geist, white on picture, one line at a time. Optional voiceover (stock preset voice).
- **Honesty:** end card always says "Coming soon · pre-alpha, not audited". No "safe", "secure",
  "unhackable", no price talk.
- **Formats:** each shot generated at 16:9; the chosen take is regenerated at 9:16. 1:1 is cut from 16:9.

## Ad 1 · "Three places" (brand anthem, 30 s) ★ recommended

The pure brand piece. Three keys, three places in an ordinary life, and no company anywhere.

| # | Shot | Super | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Early morning, a hand takes a plain black phone from a bedside table; window light | **One key in your pocket.** | 4 s |
| 2 | A wooden drawer slides open: a small matte black device rests on folded linen | **One in a drawer.** | 4 s |
| 3 | Bookshelf at dusk, plants, a small grey box with a soft white LED breathing | **One on a shelf at home.** | 5 s |
| 4 | Café, the phone face-down next to a coffee; a hand turns it over and taps (screen out of focus) → insert: real `Send-Review` UI | **Any two move your money.** | 5 s |
| 5 | Wide: the protagonist walking home across a bridge at blue hour, city lights on | **None of them belong to a company.** | 5 s |
| 6 | Logo animation (brand cut): three dots → triangle → orange dot | **Trikey. Three keys. No company.** | 4 s |
| 7 | End card | Coming soon · pre-alpha, not audited · github.com/TheIcarusWings/trikey | 3 s |

Music: the bright ambient bed, swelling on shot 5. Optional VO reads the supers.

## Ad 2 · "Seven days" (story, 45 s) ★ recommended

A small drama about losing your phone, and nobody to call because nobody needs to be called.

| # | Shot | Super | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Night train, a phone left on an empty seat; doors close; the train pulls away | **Left it on the train.** | 5 s |
| 2 | Hallway, the protagonist pats empty coat pockets, stops | **Your phone's gone. Your money isn't.** | 4 s |
| 3 | Morning kitchen table: laptop, coffee, the small black device; a thumb presses its button → insert: `Guardian-Recovery` UI | **Your hardware wallet and your own server start a recovery.** | 6 s |
| 4 | The grey box on the shelf, LED pulses once | **It waits seven days.** | 4 s |
| 5 | Time-lapse, the same window, light cycling day → night → day; supers count Day 1…Day 7 | **Day 1 · Day 2 · …** | 8 s |
| 6 | Another city: a friend's hand, a phone buzzing on a café table → insert: alert motion "Someone started a recovery" | **Every day, the people you trust get an alert.** | 5 s |
| 7 | The friend's thumb hovers over a button, then the hand relaxes and turns the phone over | **If it isn't you, anyone can stop it.** | 4 s |
| 8 | Day 7, sunlight: the protagonist unboxes a new phone at the same table → insert: `Recover-Done` UI | **Day 7. Your money moves to your new wallet.** | 5 s |
| 9 | Logo animation + end card | **No support ticket. No company. Trikey.** · Coming soon · pre-alpha, not audited | 4 s |

Music: the calm bed, low through the week, opening up on day 7.

## Ad 3 · "The burglar" (story, 30 s)

Dry humour. A burglar steals the home server and gets nothing.

| # | Shot | Super | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Night, a gloved hand lifts the grey box off the shelf, cables trailing | **Someone stole your home server.** | 5 s |
| 2 | Back of a van, the box on the floor; the gloved hand plugs it into a power bank; LED blinks | **They plug it back in.** | 5 s |
| 3 | Insert: `Guardian-Locked` UI ("Guardian locked… key 3 is encrypted") | **It locked itself the moment it lost power.** | 4 s |
| 4 | The burglar's hands drop the box on a pile of junk | **One key, locked. Useless to them.** | 4 s |
| 5 | Morning: the owner at home with phone + hardware wallet → insert: `Settings-ReplaceGuardian` | **You move to a new guardian with your other two keys.** | 6 s |
| 6 | Logo + end card | **Trikey. Three keys. No company.** | 6 s |
