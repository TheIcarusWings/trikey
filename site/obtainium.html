Obtainium installs Trikey on Android straight from its GitHub releases and tells you when an update is out. No store account, nothing in between. On your Android phone, with Obtainium installed, tap the app you want.

Add Trikey FOSS to Obtainium

The two are separate apps, each with its own wallet. You can have both.

What the button sets

  • Source: github.com/TheIcarusWings/trikey.
  • Include prereleases: on, which does no harm. New Trikey releases are normal releases; the ones up to 0.1.25-alpha are marked pre-releases.
  • Only that app's APK: ^trikey-alpha-v.*\.apk$ for Trikey, ^trikey-alpha-foss-v.*\.apk$ for Trikey FOSS.

Obtainium asks you to confirm before it adds the app. You can change any of these in its settings later.

Check who signed it

Every Trikey APK is signed with the same release key. Its certificate's SHA-256 is:

c6c46a1332725111413a7b0313e2f2f37b10f0ce4c8da1cac3c92d179a162da7

Paste it into the app's settings in Obtainium (certificate verification) so it refuses an APK signed by anything else. Android refuses such an update anyway. The same value is in the repository (release-cert.sha256) and in Trikey under Settings, About.
