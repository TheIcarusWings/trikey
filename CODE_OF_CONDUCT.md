# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Trikey a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting
- Encouraging anyone to put real funds at risk while Trikey is pre-alpha and unaudited

## Security reports

Vulnerabilities that could put funds at risk must be reported privately
([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), not in public issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers via GitHub
(Security → Report a vulnerability for security issues; otherwise open a private
contact through the repository owner's profile). Maintainers will review and act
as they judge appropriate, including warning or banning contributors.
